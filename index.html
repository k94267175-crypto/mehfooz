Is mein kya shak wali baatein hain

    Ab kya karein

    Scam hone ka yaqeen ho to number block karein aur apne mulk ke cyber crime idaray ko report karein.

    Yeh sirf ek khud-kaar mashwara hai, aakhri faisla aap ka apna hai. Demo version: abhi sirf kuch aam scam tareeqe pehchanta hai aur ghalat bhi ho sakta hai.

    Aam scam tareeqay

    • Fake BISP / Ehsaas inaam: "Aap ke Rs.75,600 aaye hain, WhatsApp par rabta karein."
    • Account block: "JazzCash / Easypaisa band ho raha hai, is link par click karein."
    • OTP ya PIN maangna: Asli bank ya wallet yeh kabhi message par nahi maangta.
    • Ghalti se paise: "Ghalti se aap ke account mein aa gaye, wapas bhej dein."
    • Dost ban kar naya number: "Emergency hai, abhi paise bhej do."
    • Ghar baithe kamai ya paise double: Pehle fee, phir paise ghayab.

    Scam report kahan karein

    • Shak wala SMS: 7726 par forward karein (PTA spam)
    • PTA helpline: 0800-55055, ya complaint.pta.gov.pk
    • Cyber crime helpline: 1991
    • Paise chale gaye hon to foran apne bank, JazzCash ya Easypaisa ki official helpline par batayein

    Yeh numbers aam websites se liye gaye hain aur badal sakte hain. Report karne se pehle PTA aur FIA/NCCIA ki official website par check kar lein.

    Mehfooz ki hadd

    Yeh abhi pehla prototype hai. Yeh khaas alfaaz aur tareeqon ke qawaid (rules) se kaam karta hai, aur naye ya ghalat likhe scam miss kar sakta hai. "Koi bara khatra nahi" ka matlab "bilkul mehfooz" nahi. Screenshot aur voice note check abhi shamil nahi.
